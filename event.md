Order of Events – Asher Turns ONE 🎂
1. Welcome & Opening Prayer 
	•	MC welcomes guests(Make everyone share their name and relationship to the celebrant)
	•	Short opening prayer (thanksgiving for baby’s life, parents, guests)

2. Baby’s Growth Video / Slideshow 
🎥 Video showing:
	•	Pregnancy / newborn days
	•	Monthly milestones
	•	First smile, crawl, steps
	•	End with “Happy 1st Birthday!”
(Soft background music works beautifully here)

5. Fun Icebreaker / Activity 1 
Choose one:
	•	“Guess Baby’s Age” using photos
	•	Baby Trivia (first word, favorite food, birth time)

Small prizes make it fun 🎁

6. Refreshments / Food Break (small chops and fruit)
	•	Guests eat & mingle
	•	Background music playing
	•	Capture candid photos

7. Cake Cutting Ceremony 
	•	Birthday song
	•	Parents help baby cut cake
	•	Wishes from guests
	•	Photos 

8. Fun Activity 2 – Games or Entertainment
Options:
	•	Short dance moment (toddler-friendly music)
	•	MC-led mini dance challenge for adults
	•	Christmas kinda competition 

9. Closing Remark & Vote of Thanks (5 mins)
	•	Quick thank-you by parents 
	•	Appreciation to guests for coming

10. Final Photos & Wind Down
	•	Family photos
	•	Guests say goodbye
